GAME GUIDE

Everything you need to know before you challenge the machine.

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Return the ball. Beat the AI.

Move your paddle to return the ball. Score points when the ball gets past the opponent. Match points can be set before starting.

DESKTOP

A / D • ← / →

Move left and right. W / S and ↑ / ↓ are also available for orientation-shift controls.

MOBILE

TOUCH BUTTONS

Use the on-screen controls. During Orientation Shift, the buttons change to ▲ / ▼.

PAUSE

ESC • Ⅱ

Pause the match, resume, restart, or exit safely.

FULLSCREEN

⛶

Use the fullscreen button during a match when your browser supports fullscreen mode.

⚡ POWER-UPS

BUFF

🔵 BIG PADDLE

Makes your paddle larger, giving you more room to intercept the ball.

DEBUFF

🔴 SMALL PADDLE

Makes your paddle smaller, making accurate returns harder.

BUFF

⚡ SPEED BOOST

Increases your paddle movement speed.

BUFF

🐌 SLOW BALL

Temporarily reduces the ball's speed.

SPECIAL

🧲 MAGNETIC BALL

Your paddle can catch the ball and hold it. Right-click or double-tap to launch. With Multi-Ball, multiple balls can be held and launched together with varied trajectories.

SPECIAL

🟣 MULTI-BALL

Creates 5 balls. ONE MISS = POINT. If even one ball gets past a paddle, the opponent immediately scores. The AI also scales its ability as more balls are active.

BUFF

🟢 BIG BALL

Makes the ball larger and easier to hit.

DEBUFF

🔴 SMALL BALL

Makes the ball smaller and harder to hit.

DEBUFF

🔄 REVERSE CONTROLS

Temporarily reverses your movement controls.

DEBUFF

💥 SCREEN SHAKE

Adds visual screen shake while the effect is active.

DEBUFF

🔥 BALL SPEED UP

Temporarily makes the ball faster.

SPECIAL

↔ ORIENTATION SHIFT

Changes the arena from vertical Pong to horizontal Pong. Your movement direction and controls adapt automatically.

MYSTERY

❓ MYSTERY

A mystery pickup gives you a random effect. You do not know what you will get until it activates.

🧠 THE MACHINE

EASY

LEARN THE RHYTHM

More forgiving movement and larger prediction error.

NORMAL

THE BALANCED FIGHT

A steady opponent with predictive movement and controlled unpredictability.

HARD

LESS ROOM FOR ERROR

Fast reactions, stronger prediction, and tighter returns.

EXTREME

NO MERCY

Highly capable predictive AI that can adapt under pressure and during Multi-Ball without becoming mathematically perfect.

TIP: Power-ups can stack. Some effects help you, some hurt you, and some completely change how the arena behaves. Learn to recognize them quickly.
